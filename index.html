Tap here

A follow-up is a reminder a salesman sets when they leave a comment on a vehicle — for example, promising to call a client back once finance is approved. This pulls every open follow-up from all three branches into one place, so nothing is missed even if the salesman who set it is off or busy. Tap the red button above to show or hide the list; click any row inside it to open that vehicle's report.

A vehicle appears here once it has been on the floor for 45 days or more without selling — usually a sign it needs a price review, a fresh push from the sales team, or a closer look at why it isn't moving. Tap the amber button above to show or hide the list; click any row inside it to open that vehicle's report.
